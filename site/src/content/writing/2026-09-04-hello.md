The split I am trying to hold:

- **Writing** is for finished pieces — things with an argument, meant to be read by
  someone who is not me.
- **Study log** is for notes as they are. No bar to clear.
- **Briefings** are not written by me at all. They are the output of a tool that
  watches what I ask about and assembles a reading queue from it.
